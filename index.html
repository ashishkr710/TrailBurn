💚 Why Open Source?

Built on Open Innovation

TrailBurn runs on open-weight AI models — your data never leaves your machine.

🔒

100% Private

Your health data, weight, and fitness goals stay on your machine. No servers, no tracking, no cloud APIs.

🌐

Works Offline

The calorie calculator works without internet. With Ollama installed, even AI coaching works fully offline.

🔄

Swap Models

Use Llama 3.2, Mistral, Phi-3, Gemma, or any model Ollama supports. Your choice, your hardware.

💰

Zero Cost

No API keys, no subscriptions, no per-token charges. Open-weight models mean truly free AI.

🧪

MET-Based Science

Calorie calculations use Metabolic Equivalent of Task (MET) values from peer-reviewed exercise physiology research.
